Display setup

In the sim, hold Right-Alt and click a screen to pop it out, then pick what each window shows. Assigned windows move to the virtual monitor, out of sight, and come back here when set to “Not used”.

Automatic pop-out

Pick a ChasePlane view that shows the screens and take a picture. Tap a screen's name, then tap that screen in the picture. The server then pops them out like Pop Out Panel Manager does (ChasePlane view, Right-Alt + click) and hides them on the virtual monitor. Keep your hands off the mouse while it runs.
